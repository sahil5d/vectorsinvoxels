### Vectors in Voxels

Model the photon and electron with vectors in voxels

why
- points and forces will not crack open the electron
- points and forces doesn't crack open the photon

draft ideas
- photonpulse. Yee cell.
  - todo, not yet like opticalbullet
- electronglobe
  - vectors on sphere and point to center
  - or on sphere and point away from center (positronglobe)
- magneticspine
  - vectors upward from point then around in circle (outward from spine can be North)
  - and around circle and back into spine (can be South)

big idea
- dot*dist operation
  - can produce 1/r energy between charges
